Stack Squeeze: Color Sort Puzzle

A colour-sort puzzle for iPhone and iPad with a twist: the board squeezes.

Move blocks between tubes until every colour is sorted — but keep an eye on the squeeze bar. When it empties, two tubes are merged and you lose a slot. Endless procedurally generated levels, a daily challenge, and a three-star par system.
