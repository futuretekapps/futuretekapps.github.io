Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

FutureTekApps ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Stack Squeeze mobile application for iOS. This app is provided as a free, ad-supported game with optional in-app purchases.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and disclosed when you use our application.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information and we operate no servers of our own. However, third-party services integrated into the app may collect certain information automatically.

This information may include:

  • Device information and operating system version
  • IP address and approximate (country-level) location
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, where you allow tracking)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewarded-video completions)
  • Crash reports and diagnostics collected by Apple, where you have opted in

Game Progress and iCloud

Your level progress, stars, gems, streak, purchases and preferences are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the same data is also stored in Apple's iCloud key-value storage so it can be restored on your other devices or after reinstalling. This data is associated with your Apple Account and is never transmitted to us. You can disable this in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Saved to iCloud, and deleting the app from all devices and turning off iCloud for the app removes it.

Advertising

Stack Squeeze displays advertisements using Google AdMob, including interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits (undo, hint, extra time, continue).

Google may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and other information necessary to provide personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure their performance.

The app uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. When you first open the app you will be asked for permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you allow tracking, Google may access your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and may link information collected in this app with third-party data to deliver and measure personalized advertising. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.

You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Purchasing Remove Ads stops interstitial ads. Rewarded videos remain available as an optional choice.

Your Consent Choices

Where required (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we request your consent for data collection and personalized ads through Google's consent interface when you first open the app. You can review or change your choice at any time via Settings › Privacy options inside the app, where this option is required in your region, or learn more at Google Ads Settings.

In-App Purchases

Purchases of gem packs and Remove Ads are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details, or billing address. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. Purchase entitlements are stored on your device and in your iCloud key-value storage as described above.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising and consent management)
  • Apple (App Store, In-App Purchase, iCloud and iOS services)

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any information collected through Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's data retention policies. Game progress lives on your device and, if enabled, in your iCloud storage, under your control.

You may request the deletion of any data associated with your use of the app at any time by contacting us at futuretekapps@gmail.com. We will process such requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law. You can also reset your advertising identifier at any time through your device's privacy settings.

Security

We value your trust and strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Stack Squeeze is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. You may exercise these rights by contacting us at futuretekapps@gmail.com. We will respond to reasonable requests in accordance with applicable law.
