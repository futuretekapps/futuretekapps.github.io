Frequently asked questions

My reminders aren't arriving

Reminders need notification permission. Open iOS Settings → TickDown → Notifications and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Inside the app, check Settings → Notifications to confirm event reminders, task reminders and summaries are enabled and that the summary time is what you expect. Also make sure a Focus mode isn't silencing them.

How do I move my data to a new device?

Open Settings → Backup & Restore and create a backup. This writes an encrypted .tickdown file containing your countdowns, tasks, subtasks, categories, event photos and settings. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then on the new device choose Restore and pick that file. You can also restore during onboarding on a fresh install.

I lost my backup passphrase

Backups are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, and we hold no copy of your key. A passphrase-protected backup cannot be recovered without its passphrase — not by us, not by anyone. Backups secured with the device key restore automatically on a device signed in to the same Apple Account.

I bought Premium but it isn't showing

Open Settings → Go Premium and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Premium is a one-time purchase — there is no subscription to renew.

Widgets aren't updating

Widgets are a Premium feature. Once Premium is active, long-press your Home or Lock Screen, add a TickDown widget, and it will refresh as time runs out. If a widget looks stale, open the app once to let it publish a fresh snapshot.

How do I stop seeing ads?

TickDown is free and ad-supported. A one-time Premium purchase removes all ads permanently and unlocks widgets and every background. You can also watch a rewarded ad for a temporary ad-free period.

How do I change my ad tracking or consent choices?

Your Apple tracking choice lives in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you're in a region where Google's consent form applies, you can change it from Settings → Ad Privacy Settings in the app.

Why does TickDown ask for calendar access?

Only so it can show your existing calendar events alongside your countdowns in the Calendar tab. It's entirely optional, read-only, and nothing is uploaded. Turn it off any time with Settings → Show Device Calendars.

How do I delete everything?

Use Settings → Delete All Data to erase your content from the device. Deleting the app also removes its local data. Backup files you saved yourself are not removed — delete those in the Files app.
