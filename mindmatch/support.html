Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
A pattern of tiles is shown for a few seconds, then hidden. Tap every tile you remember before the timer runs out. Each level grows the grid and shortens the time.
I lost my progress after reinstalling.
Progress, coins and achievements are stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes them, and they cannot be recovered.
Ads are not loading, or I see the same ad repeatedly.
Check your internet connection and reopen the app. Ad availability varies by region and time of day.
I watched a video but did not get my reward.
The reward is granted only when the video finishes. If it completed and nothing was granted, email us with the approximate time it happened.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Open Settings in the game and tap Ad Settings. This option appears where consent choices are required by law.
How do I turn notifications off?
Open Settings in the game and toggle Notifications, or use iOS Settings › Notifications › Memory Rush.
How do I report a bug?
Email us with the level number, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
