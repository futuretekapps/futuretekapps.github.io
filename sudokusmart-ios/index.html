Sudoku Master

Sudoku for iPhone and iPad that teaches you to solve.

Sudoku Master is a free Sudoku with a strong teaching focus and a one-time Premium unlock. Every hint works in three steps — where to look, why, and only then the answer — and names the technique it used. Mistakes are explained, not just marked. An illustrated Learn section covers ten techniques from scanning to X-Wing, each with a practice mode that drops you into a real puzzle right where that technique is needed.

Features

  • 9×9 and 16×16 grids, four difficulties, every puzzle verified to have a single solution
  • Three-step teaching hints and mistake explanations
  • Illustrated lessons, glossary and a solving routine; practice any technique
  • Daily challenge with streaks, statistics and achievements
  • Pencil marks, auto-notes, undo, six colour themes, light and dark mode
  • Full iPad support with landscape layouts and hardware-keyboard shortcuts
  • No account, no subscription — an optional rewarded ad for extra hints, and Premium removes all ads
