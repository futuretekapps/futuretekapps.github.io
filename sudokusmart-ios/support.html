Frequently asked questions

How do hints work?
Each hint has three steps. Step 1 tells you where to look, step 2 explains the logic, and step 3 fills in the answer (or removes pencil marks). Only step 3 costs score. Every hint names its technique and links to the lesson, so try stopping at step 1 or 2 and finishing the deduction yourself.
Why did the app say my digit is wrong?
With Check mistakes on, entries are compared with the unique solution and wrong ones turn red with an explanation — usually a clash in the row, column or box, or a cell that has only one possible candidate. Turn checking off in Settings for a paper-like experience where only direct clashes are marked.
What is the three-mistake limit?
An optional rule that ends the game after three wrong entries. Turn it off in Settings › Mistakes.
What do the letters A–G mean in 16×16?
Sixteen symbols are needed, so after 9 come A to G (A is 10, G is 16). Rows, columns and 4×4 boxes each hold all sixteen exactly once. See Learn › 16×16 Puzzles.
What is Practice mode?
Open any technique in Learn and tap Practice. The app generates a puzzle and plays it forward to a position where that technique is the next logical step, with pencil marks filled in. Ask for a hint and it looks for that technique first.
How is the score calculated?
Points per correct placement with a streak bonus, penalties for mistakes and revealed hints, a time bonus, and +20% for a flawless game and +10% for solving without hints. Full details are under Settings › How scoring works.
What is the Daily Challenge?
One 9×9 puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Difficulty rotates through the week. Complete it to extend your daily streak.
Does my progress sync between devices?
Yes. Statistics, achievements, your daily streak and the puzzle in progress sync through iCloud. Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled (iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud). If a device shows older data, open the app there and wait a few seconds.
I lost my puzzle after closing the app.
Progress is saved after every move and the Continue card on the Play tab resumes it. If you chose Abandon Puzzle when leaving, it counts as a loss and cannot be restored.
Keyboard shortcuts on iPad
Digits 1–9 and A–G enter values, arrow keys move the selection, Delete erases, N toggles Notes, H asks for a hint, ⌘Z undoes and ⇧⌘Z redoes.
What is free and what is Premium?
Free: unlimited 9×9 puzzles at every difficulty, the Daily Challenge, the Basics and Intermediate lessons with practice, and 3 explained hints a day. Premium (one-time purchase, no subscription) adds unlimited hints, the Advanced and Beyond lessons, 16×16 puzzles and removes all ads. Buy it from Settings › Go Premium or by tapping any lock.
I'm out of hints for today.
The free allowance resets at midnight. You can also watch a short rewarded video for an extra hint when the option is offered, or unlock unlimited hints with Premium. Hints in Practice mode never count towards the limit, and advancing a hint you already opened is always free.
Are there ads?
The free version has an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch for a hint — nothing plays on its own. We may add other placements later. Premium removes every ad.
I bought Premium but it's locked on my other device.
Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple Account, then tap Settings › Restore Purchases. Premium also works for Family Sharing members when sharing is enabled for purchases.
Why does the app ask to track me?
That is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, shown once before the first ad can load so Google can decide whether ads may be personalised. Choose Ask App Not to Track if you prefer; ads still work, just not personalised. Premium users never see the prompt.
How do I request a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase. Apple decides refunds; we cannot issue them ourselves.

Reporting a bug

Email us with what you expected, what happened instead, and if possible a screenshot. The ⓘ button on the game screen shows the puzzle's grid, difficulty and the techniques the solver needed — include that if a puzzle or hint seems wrong.
